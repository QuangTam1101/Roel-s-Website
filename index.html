Why "Roel"?

Back in grade 8, I was obsessed with Elon Musk. It is not just because of the cars or the rockets, but because of his mindset. People constantly doubted him, yet he kept proving them wrong. That level of boldness inspired me. One quote that always stuck with me is:
🚀
To anyone I have offended, I just want to say, I reinvented electric cars and I am sending people to Mars in a rocketship. Did you think I was also going to be a chill normal dude?
— Elon Musk
ROEL
Roof Of Elon
That energy? That unapologetic, moonshot thinking? That's what Roel means to me. The name Roel stands for "Roof of Elon." It's my way of saying I looked up to him so much, I wanted to stand on top of that inspiration - to build on it, to aim even higher.
Roel is more than a name I type into login screens. It's a quiet reminder I carry with me: if rockets can break through the pull of gravity to reach for the stars, then I can start with small steps so I can move toward dreams that once felt as distant as Mars.
